How I work

Quality engineering,
end to end.

The strongest automation is not a pile of scripts. It is a maintainable engineering system that connects product risk, business rules, delivery pipelines, and trustworthy feedback.

01

Understand the system

Map workflows, users, data, dependencies, architecture boundaries, and failure modes before deciding what to automate.

  • Requirements and acceptance criteria
  • Legacy-to-modern behavior contracts
  • Business rules and data flows
02

Design for change

Build framework layers and conventions that make new coverage easier to add, understand, debug, and maintain.

  • Page Object Model and shared operations
  • Reusable context and validation helpers
  • Environment-aware configuration
03

Integrate the feedback loop

Connect automated checks to delivery so results arrive at the moment teams can act on them.

  • Pull requests and CI/CD pipelines
  • Structured logging and reporting
  • Defect triage and fix verification
04

Communicate release risk

Turn test results into a clear view of behavior, coverage, evidence, and remaining uncertainty.

  • Traceability and test strategy
  • Root-cause investigation
  • Release-readiness decisions
